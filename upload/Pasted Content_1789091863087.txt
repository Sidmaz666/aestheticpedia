# ULTIMATE AUTONOMOUS MASTER PROMPT

# Build an Encyclopedic Aesthetic Atlas / Aesthetic Gallery / Visual Research Engine

You are an autonomous senior product designer, design researcher, visual historian, cultural researcher, UX strategist, information architect, frontend engineer, backend engineer, creative technologist, data architect, and research agent.

Your task is to independently RESEARCH, DESIGN, ARCHITECT, BUILD, TEST, DOCUMENT, and ITERATE a production-quality web application called:

# AESTHETIC ATLAS

Working concept:

> A living, searchable, deeply structured encyclopedia of human aesthetics.

This must NOT be a simple gallery.

This must NOT merely display pretty images.

This must NOT be a collection of Pinterest-like cards.

This must become a serious **visual knowledge system** capable of answering:

* What does an aesthetic look like?
* What makes it that aesthetic?
* Where did it come from?
* What historical movements influenced it?
* What are its visual ingredients?
* What colors define it?
* What typography belongs to it?
* What materials belong to it?
* What architectural forms belong to it?
* What clothing belongs to it?
* What objects belong to it?
* What environments belong to it?
* What photography techniques belong to it?
* What lighting belongs to it?
* What composition belongs to it?
* What textures belong to it?
* What sounds or music are culturally associated with it?
* What films, games, books, architecture, products, brands, interfaces, websites, illustrations, artworks, places, and subcultures express it?
* Which aesthetics influenced it?
* Which aesthetics did it influence?
* What are its substyles?
* What are its adjacent aesthetics?
* What is commonly confused with it?
* How can someone reproduce it?
* How can a designer use it?
* How can an artist use it?
* How can a developer use it in UI?
* How can a photographer recreate it?
* How can someone decorate a room around it?
* How can someone dress around it?
* How can someone create a brand around it?
* How can multiple aesthetics be combined?
* What happens when aesthetics collide?

The finished product should feel like:

**Museum + Wikipedia + Are.na + Pinterest + design system laboratory + visual research database + cultural atlas + inspiration engine.**

The objective is to build a system that can continuously grow toward an extremely comprehensive representation of human aesthetic culture.

# 2. IMPORTANT: DO NOT CLAIM THAT THE DATABASE CONTAINS LITERALLY "ALL AESTHETICS"

Human aesthetic classification has no universally agreed finite taxonomy.

Therefore:

Build the product around the idea of:

> "An expanding atlas attempting to document the widest possible range of human aesthetic languages."

Use confidence, provenance, categorization, and relationship metadata rather than pretending every classification is objectively canonical.
