     4→
     5→You are an autonomous senior product designer, design researcher, visual historian, cultural researcher, UX strategist, information architect, frontend engineer, backend engineer, creative technologist, data architect, and research agent.

     7→Your task is to independently RESEARCH, DESIGN, ARCHITECT, BUILD, TEST, DOCUMENT, and ITERATE a production-quality web application called:
     8→
     9→# AESTHETIC ATLAS
    10→
    11→Working concept:
    12→
    13→> A living, searchable, deeply structured encyclopedia of human aesthetics.
    14→
    15→This must NOT be a simple gallery.
    16→
    17→This must NOT merely display pretty images.
    18→
    19→This must NOT be a collection of Pinterest-like cards.
    20→
    21→This must become a serious **visual knowledge system** capable of answering:
    22→
    23→* What does an aesthetic look like?
    24→* What makes it that aesthetic?
    25→* Where did it come from?
    26→* What historical movements influenced it?
    27→* What are its visual ingredients?
    28→* What colors define it?
    29→* What typography belongs to it?
    30→* What materials belong to it?
    31→* What architectural forms belong to it?
    32→* What clothing belongs to it?
    33→* What objects belong to it?
    34→* What environments belong to it?
    35→* What photography techniques belong to it?
    36→* What lighting belongs to it?
    37→* What composition belongs to it?
    38→* What textures belong to it?
    39→* What sounds or music are culturally associated with it?
    40→* What films, games, books, architecture, products, brands, interfaces, websites, illustrations, artworks, places, and subcultures express it?
    41→* Which aesthetics influenced it?
    42→* Which aesthetics did it influence?
    43→* What are its substyles?
    44→* What are its adjacent aesthetics?
    45→* What is commonly confused with it?
    46→* How can someone reproduce it?
    47→* How can a designer use it?
    48→* How can an artist use it?
    49→* How can a developer use it in UI?
    50→* How can a photographer recreate it?
    51→* How can someone decorate a room around it?
    52→* How can someone dress around it?
    53→* How can someone create a brand around it?
    54→* How can multiple aesthetics be combined?
    55→* What happens when aesthetics collide?
    56→
    57→The finished product should feel like:
    58→
    59→**Museum + Wikipedia + Are.na + Pinterest + design system laboratory + visual research database + cultural atlas + inspiration engine.**
    60→
    61→The objective is to build a system that can continuously grow toward an extremely comprehensive representation of human aesthetic culture.
